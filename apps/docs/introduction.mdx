## Data retention

On useSend cloud, the content of each email you send (HTML, text, attachments and headers) is kept for 90 days after sending, then deleted. Delivery status, events and analytics are kept for as long as your account exists. After the content is removed, the dashboard shows that it was removed, and the API returns `null` for `html` and `text`.

Self-hosting? You choose the retention period with `EMAIL_CLEANUP_DAYS`. See [self-hosting](/self-hosting/overview).
